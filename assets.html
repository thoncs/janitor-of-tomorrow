🧹 JANITOR OF TOMORROW — asset catalog

Every sprite and drawn entity in the game, with its code hook. Generated from the live build.

To replace an image asset: produce a PNG at (or near) the native size with a transparent background, then swap its data URI in index.html:
python3 -c "import base64,re;u='data:image/png;base64,'+base64.b64encode(open('new.png','rb').read()).decode();s=open('index.html').read();open('index.html','w').write(re.sub(r\"KEY:'data:image/png;base64,[^']*'\",f\"KEY:'{u}'\",s))"
where KEY is the hook shown on the card (e.g. scr_blob). Small sprites are drawn scaled with smoothing off — chunky pixels welcome. Hit-flash variants are generated automatically.
